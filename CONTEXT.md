# Societies Context Capsule

Societies is a deterministic civilization and ecology simulation. Snow Globe is its first deliberately small, complete realization: a bounded world where an embodied human and persistent citizens should eventually work, negotiate, refuse, remember commitments, and live with shared material and ecological consequences.

## Historical shape

- **Pre-V3:** broad, fragmented experimentation. It produced useful concepts and code, but development expanded horizontally without converging on the product.
- **V3:** first dependable deterministic Godot/C# substrate, with stronger testing, persistence, replay, and evidence discipline.
- **Snow Globe:** first unified architecture around persistent agents, bounded observations, deterministic proposals, provider-neutral cognition, recovery, and replay.
- **EB-01R:** first user-accepted bounded player-facing worldcraft slice. It improves the world, HUD, inventory, construction, and interaction surface, but still contains no participating citizens.
- **Consolidation:** current phase. One repository, one authority chain, one active milestone, historical work archived, and feature work paused until the next product proof is selected.

## Permanent direction

The deterministic simulation owns reality. LLMs may help citizens interpret, deliberate, communicate, summarize bounded memory, and propose closed actions, but every world change is validated and replayable. The human remains consequential. Citizens remain autonomous enough to counter, refuse, delay, withdraw, or act without the player for legible reasons.

The first mature product proof remains a small wetland settlement, an embodied resident-founder, three materially distinct citizens, and a negotiated water-control consequence that changes ecology, labor, resources, trust, and the next day.
