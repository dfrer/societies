# Workflow Issues

The canonical live risk and debt register is [`docs/project/RISKS_AND_DEBT.md`](docs/project/RISKS_AND_DEBT.md). Do not append agent-session narratives here.

The central process risks are:

- agents selecting adjacent technical work instead of the next product proof;
- automated success being mistaken for human acceptance;
- multiple active plans and stacked branches creating conflicting authority;
- infrastructure growing faster than citizen and player experience;
- unverified completion summaries replacing direct diff, test, runtime, and human evidence.

The consolidation milestone addresses the authority and repository portions. Product, performance, and code-structure risks remain explicit until a bounded milestone resolves them.
