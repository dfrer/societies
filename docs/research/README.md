# Research Navigation and Adoption

Most source research currently lives under `planning/research/`, with concept and session material under adjacent planning directories. This index defines how research becomes project authority.

Research may:

- provide evidence about games, simulation, cognition, ecology, UX, performance, or tools;
- challenge assumptions;
- propose product or technical options;
- supply references and experiments.

Research does **not** automatically:

- change the charter;
- activate a milestone;
- become accepted architecture;
- prove a provider/model choice;
- justify feature breadth.

Adoption requires one of:

1. an owner decision recorded in `docs/project/DECISION_LOG.md`;
2. an accepted ADR for technical architecture; or
3. explicit citation in the active milestone with a bounded claim and evidence requirement.

Research documents should record source, date, question, findings, limitations, and project implication. Separate external fact, interpretation, recommendation, and speculation.
