## Interview Discipline

- Ask three to five connected questions at a time.
- Prefer concrete situations and tradeoffs over abstract preference polling.
- Do not convert ambiguity into an Accepted decision.
- After each answer set, summarize what was learned and what remains unresolved.
- End each topic with explicit approval, revision, rejection, or deferral choices.
